# Team Contract — Queue Buddy (Team Cookies and Cream)

## Team Leadership & Roles
- **Karim — Team Lead.** Coordinates the project, tracks goals and deadlines, and keeps the team aligned.
- **Ayo — Technical Lead.** Owns code quality, best practices, and the code-review process.
- **Oscar — Design Lead.** Manages the app's design and user-facing delivery.
- **Andrew, Amy, and Marco — Team Members.** Contribute to discussions, planning, implementation, and testing.

## Communication
- Our team will use Discord to communicate about project-related matters.
- Important updates, decisions, and blockers will be posted in the group chat so that everyone can stay informed.
- Team members are expected to respond to project-related messages within a reasonable time.

## Meetings
- Our team will meet every week at a time agreed upon by all members.
- All members will attend meetings or notify the team in advance if they cannot attend. We agree that absences should be rare.
- All members will come to meetings prepared by reading any assigned material and bringing updates, questions, or ideas related to the project.
- Members will stay engaged during meetings and will not work on unrelated assignments during meeting time.
- The team lead will help prepare the agenda and keep the meeting on track.
- Someone will be designated to take notes during each meeting, and the notes will be shared with the team after the meeting.

## Team and Individual Expectations
- Each member is expected to complete assigned tasks by the agreed deadline.
- If a member is blocked or cannot finish a task on time, they should tell the team early.
- Everyone is expected to contribute to discussions, planning, implementation, and testing.
- Team members should ask for help when needed and support each other when possible.
- Code should be reviewed before being merged.

## Decision Making
- Major project decisions will be made with input from the whole team.
- We will try to make decisions by consensus whenever possible.
- If the team cannot fully agree, we will discuss the options and choose the decision that best supports the project requirements, timeline, and overall quality.

## Conflict Management
- We understand that conflict can happen during team projects, and we will treat conflicts as problems to solve together rather than as one person against another.
- We will first try to resolve issues through respectful and direct discussion.
- We will focus on accommodating, compromising, and collaborating when disagreements happen.
- If needed, we will bring the issue to the whole team and work toward a fair solution.
- The goal is for the team to succeed together.
